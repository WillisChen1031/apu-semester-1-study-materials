# 00 Module Introduction

## Module Aim

- **Digital Thinking (数字思维)** is a fundamental shift from traditional ways of working and learning to more agile, adaptive ways supported by emerging digital technologies.  
  中文：这门课强调从传统工作/学习方式转向更敏捷、更能适应数字技术变化的思维。
- The module shifts students' **Systems Thinking (系统思维)** toward early-stage **Innovation (创新)**.  
  中文：你需要学会从系统整体看问题，并把这种思维用于创新。
- Agile thinking and innovation help students adapt to a **VUCA environment (volatile, uncertain, complex, ambiguous environment / 易变、不确定、复杂、模糊的环境)**.  
  中文：VUCA 是商业和技术环境变化快、不确定性高的意思。

## Course Learning Outcomes

- Explain the role of **Digital Mindset (数字心态)**, **Digital Culture (数字文化)**, and **Digital Innovation (数字创新)** in a rapidly changing business environment.
- Demonstrate the application of digital thinking to promote digital innovation in an organization.
- Study the adoption of digital innovation trends and their associated challenges in industry.

## Assessment Reminder

- **Exam (考试)**: 50%, mainly CLO1.
- **Incourse (平时作业)**: 50%, including individual and group assignments.
- Course content for exam includes Digital Generation, Agile and Digital Thinking, Digital Culture and Society, Systems Thinking, Digital Trends, AI, Analytics, IoE, Digital Currencies and Blockchain, Digital Security, and Digital Ethics.

## Revision Strategy

- Treat the course as a chain: digital generation creates new behavior; agile and digital thinking explain how to respond; culture and society explain impact; systems thinking explains how to analyze complexity; trends, AI, analytics, IoE, blockchain, security, and ethics explain specific innovation areas.
- 中文理解：这门课不是背零散概念，而是背“数字时代的人 -> 思维方式 -> 社会变化 -> 组织创新 -> 技术趋势 -> 风险与伦理”这条线。
