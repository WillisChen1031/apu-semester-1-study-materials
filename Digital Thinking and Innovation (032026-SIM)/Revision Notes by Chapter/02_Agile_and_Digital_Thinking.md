# 02 Agile and Digital Thinking

## Agile Thinking

- **Agile Thinking (敏捷思维)** is the way of thinking that enables a person or organization to respond to change or solve problems quickly and easily.  
  中文：敏捷思维就是快速回应变化、快速解决问题的思维。
- It treats everything as lessons, adjusts actions according to feedback, and moves toward desired outcomes through **continuous improvement (持续改进)**.
- **Exam Focus - Fill-in**: Flexibility and adaptability are key characteristics of **agile (敏捷)** thinking.
- **Exam Focus - Fill-in**: The Agile mindset includes a willingness to fail, viewing failures as opportunities for **learning (学习)**.
- **Exam Focus - Fill-in**: The combination of agile and digital thinking allows businesses to innovate continuously and respond to **change (变化)** quickly.
- **Practice Focus - Short Answer**: Explain Agile Thinking.

## Agile Mindset

- **Positive attitude (积极态度)**: be open to change and improvement.
- **Thirst for knowledge (求知欲)**: believe there is always something new to learn.
- **Goal of team success (团队成功目标)**: focus on collective results, not just individual output.
- **Pragmatism (实用主义)**: choose practical solutions that work in real situations.
- **Willingness to fail (愿意失败)**: accept failure as a path to learning and improvement.

- **Practice Focus - Short Answer**: Explain the characteristics that make up the agile mindset.

## Digital Thinking

- **Digital Thinking (数字思维)** is the variety of thinking skills that comprise **Digital Literacy (数字素养)**.  
  中文：数字思维是组成数字素养的一系列思考能力。
- It helps people transform real-life challenges into structured problems that can be solved through **computer-based knowledge (基于计算机的知识)**.
- Digital thinking uses data, digital tools, platforms, automation, and user-centered problem solving to create value.
- **Exam Focus - Fill-in**: Digital thinking empowers businesses to use data for **data-driven (数据驱动的)** decision-making.
- **Exam Focus - Fill-in**: Digital thinking enables transformation of real-life challenges into structured problems solvable through **computer-based (基于计算机的)** knowledge.
- **Practice Focus - Short Answer**: Explain Digital Thinking.

## Digital Literacy

- **Digital Literacy (数字素养)** is an individual's ability to find, evaluate, and compose clear information through writing and other media on digital platforms.  
  中文：数字素养是能在数字平台寻找、判断、整理和表达信息的能力。
- **Exam Focus - Fill-in / MCQ**: Ability to find, evaluate, and compose information on digital platforms = **Digital Literacy (数字素养)**.

## Collaboration and Customer-Centric Work

- **Collaboration (协作)** and communication are essential components of agile and digital thinking because they support teamwork and **innovation (创新)**.
- Agile thinking promotes a **customer-centric approach (以客户为中心的方法)**: collect feedback, understand pain points, and improve products based on user needs.
- **Exam Focus - Fill-in**: Collaboration and communication foster teamwork and **innovation**.
- **Exam Focus - Fill-in**: Agile thinking promotes a **customer-centric (以客户为中心)** approach.

## Design Thinking and Agile in Practice

- Innovation is not only about new technology; it is also about new models of organization.
- **Design Thinking (设计思维)** is a method of thinking that supports collaboration, conversation, and co-creation.
- Airbnb is used as a case study: it stands out through product positioning, personalization, social media engagement, and community building.
- **Exam Focus - MCQ**: Airbnb founders solving rental problems through innovation can be linked to **Agile Thinking**.
