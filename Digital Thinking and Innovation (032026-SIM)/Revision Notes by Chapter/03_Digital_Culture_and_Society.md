# 03 Digital Culture and Society

## Culture and Culture Change

- **Culture (文化)** is symbolic communication accepted by a community. It is a way of life of a group of people, including behaviors, beliefs, and values passed across generations through communication and imitation.
- **Culture Change (文化变迁)** can occur from within through new philosophical ideas or through **technological advancement (技术进步)**.
- Technology changes how people shop, research, access news, use healthcare, learn, and entertain themselves.
- **Exam Focus - Fill-in**: Cultural change can occur through new philosophical ideas or technological **advancements (进步)**.
- **Exam Focus - Fill-in**: Technology today has changed the way people do almost **everything (几乎所有事情)**.

## Digital Culture

- **Digital Culture (数字文化)** describes how technology and the internet shape how humans interact, behave, think, and communicate within society.  
  中文：数字文化就是科技和互联网塑造人的互动、行为、思考和沟通方式。
- It is the product of persuasive technology and disruptive technological innovation.
- Examples include **digital payment (数字支付)**, **online shopping (网上购物)**, **online services (线上服务)**, **online education (线上教育)**, **GPS navigation (GPS 导航)**, streaming news, digital entertainment, and social connection.
- **Exam Focus - Fill-in**: In digital culture, accessibility of information is enhanced through the **internet (互联网)**.
- **Exam Focus - Fill-in**: Social media platforms have changed how we **communicate (沟通)**.
- **Exam Focus - Fill-in**: Payment methods have evolved to include digital wallets and **cryptocurrencies (加密货币)**.

## Digital Society

- **Digital Society (数字社会)** is a progressive society formed through the adaptation and integration of advanced technologies into society and culture.
- Engaging in the digital world has become part of everyday life, but there is a tipping point between benefits and overuse.
- **Digital Native (数字原住民)**: a person who grew up in the digital age.
- **Digital Immigrant (数字移民)**: a person who became familiar with digital systems as an adult.
- **Digital Dependency (数字依赖)**: overuse of the internet or electronics to the point that daily life is affected.
- **Exam Focus - Fill-in**: A digitally native person is different from a digital **immigrant (移民)**.
- **Practice Focus - MCQ**: Digital dependency means overuse of the internet or electronics until daily life is affected.
- **Practice Focus - Short Answer**: Explain Digital Society.

## Multitasking and Emotional Impact

- **Multitasking (多任务处理)** means using or engaging with multiple devices or tasks at once.
- Many Gen Z users use multiple devices at one time. This can increase stress and reduce filtering of irrelevant information, but some users feel it helps them work in chaotic environments.
- Online identity affects emotional health, but digital technology can also provide emotional support through social media, crisis chat, and online communities.
- **Exam Focus - Fill-in**: People often use multiple devices at once, engaging in **multitasking (多任务处理)**.

## Digital Culture in Healthcare

- **Exam Focus - Short Answer**: Explain two benefits of digital culture on modern healthcare services and one example.
- Benefit 1: **Improved access to healthcare (改善医疗可及性)** through telemedicine, online consultation, and health apps.
- Benefit 2: **Faster communication and information sharing (更快沟通和信息共享)** through digital patient records, test results, and treatment updates.
- Example: A rural patient consults a doctor through a video call or telemedicine app without travelling to the hospital.
