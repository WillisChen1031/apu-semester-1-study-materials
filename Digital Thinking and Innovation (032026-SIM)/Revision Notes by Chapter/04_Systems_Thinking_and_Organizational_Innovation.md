# 04 Systems Thinking and Organizational Innovation

## Systems Thinking

- **Systems Thinking (系统思维)** is an approach to understanding a system by examining the linkages and interactions between the elements that form the whole system.  
  中文：系统思维不是只看单个部分，而是看部分之间如何互相影响。
- The component parts of a system may act differently when isolated from the system's environment or other parts.
  中文：系统里的部分单独拿出来时，表现可能和在系统里不同。
- **Exam Focus - MCQ**: Systems thinking means understanding interactions between elements.
  中文：选择题看到 interactions between elements，就是 Systems Thinking。
- **Exam Focus - MCQ**: In organizations, systems thinking helps identify **root causes (根本原因)**.
  中文：组织问题中，系统思维帮助找根本原因，不只处理表面问题。

## Systems Thinking vs Traditional Analysis

- **Traditional analysis (传统分析)** breaks problems into smaller parts. This is useful, but limited if it ignores relationships.
  中文：传统分析把问题拆小，适合简单问题，但容易忽略部分之间的关系。
- Traditional analysis may assume the world stands still, situations stay unchanged, and relationships between pieces are not important.
  中文：传统分析的限制是把现实看得太静态，好像环境不会变。
- Systems thinking complements analysis by seeing wholes, relationships, patterns, feedback, and long-term effects.
  中文：系统思维补足传统分析，强调整体、关系、模式、反馈和长期影响。
- **Exam Focus - MCQ**: A limitation of traditional analysis is that it ignores interactions between components.
  中文：traditional analysis 的缺点：忽略 components 之间的 interactions。
- **Exam Focus - MCQ**: Traditional analysis focuses on static snapshots, while systems thinking considers dynamic relationships.
  中文：traditional = 静态快照；systems thinking = 动态关系。

## Why Systems Thinking Matters

- It helps people see the world in terms of wholes rather than isolated events.
  中文：帮助我们看整体，而不是只看单个事件。
- It shows how parts of a system work together.
  中文：说明各部分如何一起运作。
- It explains how relationships influence behavior and events.
  中文：解释关系如何影响行为和结果。
- It recognizes that life is always moving and changing.
  中文：承认现实一直在变化，不是静止的。
- It helps people understand delayed effects, where one event influences another later or far away.
  中文：帮助理解延迟影响：一个事件可能过一段时间、在远处产生后果。
- It challenges **mental models (心智模式)**, meaning assumptions about how the world works.
  中文：挑战我们对世界运行方式的默认假设。
- It considers both short-term and long-term impact.
  中文：同时考虑短期和长期影响。

## Basic Characteristics of a System

- **Heap vs System (堆 vs 系统)**: In a heap, removing or adding parts may not change the whole much. In a system, changing a part can change how the whole works.  
  中文：一碗坚果少几个还是坚果；汽车少了电池就无法启动。
- **The whole is greater than the sum of its parts (整体大于部分之和)**: interactions among parts create **emergent properties (涌现特性)** that cannot be understood by adding parts separately.  
  中文：汽车零件相加不等于“速度”和“舒适度”；团队合作也会产生额外效果。
- **Purpose (目的)**: most systems have a purpose, and subsystems may have conflicting purposes.
  中文：系统通常有目的；子系统之间的目的有时会冲突。
- **Feedback loops (反馈回路)**: causes and effects may form a circle, where A affects B, B affects C, and C affects A.
  中文：因果可能不是直线，而是循环：A 影响 B，B 影响 C，C 又影响 A。
- **Systems archetypes (系统原型)**: different situations can follow similar patterns, such as escalation in arguments or price wars.
  中文：不同场景可能有相似模式，例如吵架升级和价格战升级。
- **Exam Focus - MCQ**: "The whole is greater than the sum of its parts" means interactions create emergent properties.
  中文：整体大于部分之和，意思是互动产生了单个部分没有的新特性。
- **Exam Focus - MCQ**: Understanding a system's purpose provides insight into system functionality and possible improvement.
  中文：理解系统目的，可以看懂系统为什么这样运作，以及如何改进。

## Systems Thinking for Organizations

- Systems thinking helps organizations understand and analyze changing contexts.
  中文：帮助组织理解不断变化的环境。
- It allows programs to be redesigned when ground conditions change.
  中文：当实际条件变化时，项目或方案可以重新设计。
- It brings together stakeholders from different backgrounds and perspectives.
  中文：能把不同背景和观点的利益相关者聚在一起。
- It helps identify problems and support transformational change.
  中文：帮助发现问题，并推动转型变化。
- Benefits include exploring new business opportunities, creating a future vision, understanding human factors in change, and redesigning broken systems.
  中文：好处包括找新商机、建立未来愿景、理解变化中的人为因素、重设计坏掉的系统。

## Sustainable Development Goals

- Organizations can use systems thinking at three levels:
  中文：组织可从三个层次使用系统思维：
- **Level 1: Joined-up efforts on individual goals (单个目标的协同努力)**.
  中文：第一层是围绕单个目标进行协作。
- **Level 2: A network set of goals (目标网络)**, looking at relationships among goals.
  中文：第二层是看目标之间的相互关系。
- **Level 3: The how of sustainable development (可持续发展的执行方式)**, delivering goals in a way that models a sustainable society.
  中文：第三层是用可持续的方式实现目标。
