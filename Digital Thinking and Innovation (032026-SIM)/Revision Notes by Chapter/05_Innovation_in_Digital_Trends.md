## Digitization of Business

- **Digitization (数字化)** reshapes business by using digital technologies to transform processes, communication, customer engagement, and economic activity.
  中文：数字化会改变企业流程、沟通方式、客户互动和经济活动。
- Social media and internet platforms remove time and space barriers, allowing global communication and real-time business interaction.
  中文：社交媒体和互联网平台打破时间与空间限制，让企业可以全球沟通、实时互动。
- Digital technologies create closer links between humans, between things, and between humans and things.
  中文：数字技术让人与人、物与物、人与物之间连接更紧密。
- **Exam Focus - MCQ**: A business shifting from physical stores to online platforms is **digitization (数字化)**.
  中文：实体店转线上平台，就是 business process digitization。
- **Exam Focus - MCQ**: Digitization affects individuals, companies, and countries by transforming processes and interactions.
  中文：数字化的整体影响是改变流程、沟通和经济活动。

## Kodak Case Study

- Kodak was a photography and film giant and even created the first digital camera using **CCD / Charge Coupled Device (电荷耦合器件)**.
  中文：Kodak 曾是胶片巨头，还发明过数字相机核心技术 CCD。
- Kodak failed because it did not keep abreast of digital technologies and did not adapt quickly enough to disruptive change.
  中文：Kodak 失败不是因为没有技术，而是没有及时拥抱数字化商业模式。
- **Practice Focus - Short Answer**: Explain why Kodak did not make it through and what it could have done to keep up with digital technologies.
  中文：简答题要写“没有跟上数字技术、抗拒转型、商业模式落后”。
- Good answer angle: Kodak should have invested earlier in digital photography, cloud/photo storage, online sharing, mobile integration, and new business models.
  中文：可答：更早投入数码摄影、云相册、在线分享、手机整合、新商业模式。

## Digital Business World

- Leaders in the digital age can fall behind quickly when they resist reform.
  中文：数字时代领先企业如果拒绝改革，也会很快落后。
- Examples from the slides: Microsoft, Intel, HP, and Yahoo were overtaken in influence by Apple, Google, Amazon, and Facebook; Nokia and Blackberry declined after failing to adapt.
  中文：课件例子说明：不适应数字趋势的公司会被新平台型公司超越。
- Internet-inspired market players often use platforms, data, mobile apps, and cloud infrastructure to scale faster.
  中文：互联网型企业常靠平台、数据、移动 app 和云基础设施快速扩张。

## Digital Trends in the Slides

- **Big Data (大数据)**: using new thinking to interpret data with the help of the Internet, not merely focusing on data volume.
  中文：大数据重点不是“数据很多”本身，而是用新思维解释数据、发现价值。
- **Cloud Computing (云计算)**: an environment that provides resources and services through the Internet.
  中文：云计算是通过互联网提供计算资源和服务。
- **Mobile Internet (移动互联网)**: mobile access reshapes lifestyle, navigation, transport, news, games, shopping, and media.
  中文：移动互联网通过手机改变导航、交通、新闻、游戏、购物和媒体消费。
- **Software-Defined Anything / SDx (软件定义一切)**: software controls and virtualizes hardware infrastructure such as storage, security, and networks.
  中文：SDx 是用软件控制硬件基础设施，例如存储、安全、网络。
- **Outsourcing and Crowdsourcing (外包与众包)**: using outside parties or online crowds to complete work.
  中文：外包找第三方做事；众包找大量网络用户共同参与。
- **Prosumer Economy (产消者经济)**: consumer demand leads the demand chain; a prosumer both consumes and produces.
  中文：消费者不只是买，也会参与生产、评价、传播和创造内容。
- **Long Tail (长尾理论)**: selling many low-volume niche products can create value, not only selling a few best-sellers.
  中文：不只卖爆款，大量小众产品加起来也能创造利润。
- **Digital Finance (数字金融)**: digital payment and financial technologies connect merchants, banks, and users.
  中文：数字金融用数字支付和金融技术连接商家、银行、用户。
- **Collaboration (协作)**: people contribute expertise toward a shared objective.
  中文：协作是多人贡献各自专业能力，一起完成共同目标。
- **Practice Focus - Short Answer**: If the question asks for seven digital trends, group them by focus: infrastructure, productivity, production organization, economic growth, competitive landscape, financial entities and technologies, and operation models.
  中文：如果题目问七类趋势，就按影响领域背：基础设施、生产力、生产组织、经济增长、竞争格局、金融实体与技术、运营模式。

## Big Data, Cloud, and Mobile Internet

- **Big Data Analytics (大数据分析)** is used by Spotify and Amazon Prime to analyze user behavior and recommend music, videos, books, or products.
  中文：Spotify 和 Amazon Prime 用大数据分析用户行为，再做个性化推荐。
- **Cloud Computing (云计算)** includes computing over the Internet, virtualized platforms, scalable resources, and pay-per-use.
  中文：云计算关键词：互联网计算、虚拟化平台、可扩展资源、按使用付费。
- **Mobile Internet (移动互联网)** includes Waze for navigation, Grab for taxis, and mobile browsing for news, games, movies, and social media.
  中文：移动互联网例子：Waze 导航、Grab 打车、手机看新闻/游戏/电影/社媒。
- **Exam Focus - Fill-in / MCQ**: Cloud computing provides resources and services via the **Internet (互联网)**.
  中文：题目出现 resources and services via Internet，答案是 Cloud Computing。

## Software-Defined Anything

- **Software-Defined Anything / SDx (软件定义一切)** uses software to control hardware infrastructure.
  中文：SDx 的重点是“用软件控制硬件”。
- It supports virtualization of technology stacks, including storage, security, and networks.
  中文：它可以虚拟化存储、安全、网络等技术层。
- Examples of software-defined disruption include Amazon as a bookseller platform, Netflix as a video service, and iTunes as a music platform.
  中文：例子说明软件平台可以颠覆传统行业：Amazon 书店、Netflix 视频、iTunes 音乐。

## Outsourcing and Crowdsourcing

- **Outsourcing (外包)** means hiring a third party to perform tasks, operations, or services that would otherwise be done internally.
  中文：外包就是把原本公司内部做的工作交给外部第三方。
- Main idea: "focus on what you do best and let others do the rest."
  中文：核心句：专注自己最擅长的，其他交给别人。
- Reasons include reducing costs, improving efficiency, accessing expertise, shifting resources to core business, and using regional specialization.
  中文：外包原因：降成本、提效率、获得专业能力、集中资源做核心业务、利用地区专业优势。
- **Crowdsourcing (众包)** means obtaining information, ideas, or services from a large group of people through online platforms.
  中文：众包是通过网络平台向大量人收集信息、想法或服务。
- Examples: Tomnod crowdsourced MH370 image tagging, Waze uses user map edits, Applause uses crowdtesters, and Wikipedia uses collective knowledge.
  中文：例子：Tomnod 找网友标记 MH370 卫星图；Waze 靠用户改地图；Wikipedia 靠集体知识。
- **Exam Focus - Fill-in / MCQ**: "Focus on what you do best and let others do the rest" = **Outsourcing (外包)**.
  中文：这句原话常考，答案就是 Outsourcing。
- **Exam Focus - Fill-in**: Leveraging collective intelligence through online platforms = **Crowdsourcing (众包)**.
  中文：collective intelligence + online platforms 对应 Crowdsourcing。

## Prosumer Economy

- **Prosumer Economy (产消者经济)** means consumer demand plays a leading role and reshapes the supply chain into a demand chain.
  中文：产消者经济里，消费者需求主导链条，供应链变成需求链。
- **Prosumer (产消者)** combines provider and consumer: a person who both consumes and produces.
  中文：prosumer = producer/provider + consumer，既消费也生产。
- **Exam Focus - Fill-in**: Consumer demand plays a leading role in the demand chain = **Prosumer Economy**.
  中文：consumer demand plays a leading role 这句对应 Prosumer Economy。

## Long Tail and Economies of Scale

- **Long Tail (长尾理论)** refers to a large number of products that sell in small quantities, compared with a small number of best-selling products.
  中文：长尾指很多小众产品每个卖得少，但整体加起来也有价值。
- Long tail strategy: provide customers with all products and help them find what they need.
  中文：长尾策略口诀：提供所有产品，并帮助顾客找到它。
- **Economies of scale (规模经济)** means average cost decreases as production increases because fixed costs are spread over more units.
  中文：规模经济就是产量越大，固定成本分摊到每件产品越低。
- **Diseconomies of scale (规模不经济)** occurs when profits decrease with expanded production.
  中文：规模不经济是扩产后反而利润下降或效率变差。
- **Exam Focus - Fill-in**: Concept focusing on changes in competitive landscape = **Long Tail**.
  中文：competitive landscape 的变化，常考答案是 Long Tail。

## Digital Finance and Collaboration

- **Digital Finance (数字金融)** uses digital technologies for payment, settlement, and financial services. Examples include PayPal, Alipay, and Google Wallet.
  中文：数字金融用数字技术做支付、结算和金融服务，例子有 PayPal、Alipay、Google Wallet。
- Third-party payment companies build bridges between merchants and banks.
  中文：第三方支付公司把商家和银行系统连接起来。
- **Collaboration (协作)** means a group of people contribute expertise for a shared objective, project, or mission.
  中文：协作是多人为了共同目标贡献各自专业能力。
- Tools include Google Drive, Google Meet, Skype, Microsoft Teams, OneDrive, SharePoint, and Yammer.
  中文：协作工具例子：Google Drive、Meet、Skype、Teams、OneDrive、SharePoint。
- **Exam Focus - Fill-in**: Changes in financial entities and technologies = **Digital Finance**.
  中文：financial entities and technologies 的变化，对应 Digital Finance。
- **Exam Focus - Fill-in**: Shared expertise toward a common goal = **Collaboration**.
  中文：shared expertise + common goal，对应 Collaboration。
