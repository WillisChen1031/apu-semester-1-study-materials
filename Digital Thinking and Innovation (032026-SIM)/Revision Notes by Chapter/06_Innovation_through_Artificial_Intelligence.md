# 06 Innovation through Artificial Intelligence

## Artificial Intelligence

- **Artificial Intelligence / AI (人工智能)** is the concept of creating computer programs or machines capable of behavior we would regard as intelligent if shown by humans.  
  中文：AI 是让机器表现出类似人类智能行为的技术。
- AI affects livelihoods, labor, decision-making, and even people's sense of self.
- **Practice Focus - Short Answer**: Explain Artificial Intelligence.

## Expert System

- **Expert System / ES (专家系统)** is a system that captures human expert knowledge in a computer to solve problems that normally require human expertise.
- It imitates an expert's reasoning process to solve specific problems.
- Key idea: problem solving through **thinking (思考)**, **perception (感知)**, and **action (行动)**.
- **Exam Focus - MCQ**: An expert system is designed to mimic human expert decision-making.
- **Practice Focus - Short Answer**: Explain the concept of expert systems.

## Why Use Expert Systems

- To preserve expert knowledge.
- To help when expertise is scarce, expensive, unavailable, or needed under time pressure.
- To train new employees.
- To improve worker productivity.
- **Rules and Inferencing (规则与推理)**: expert systems use IF-THEN rules, such as "IF face is pale AND pulse is accelerated, THEN patient is in shock."
- **Explanation Capability (解释能力)**: the system should explain why it reached a conclusion.

## Machine Learning

- **Machine Learning / ML (机器学习)** is a method of data analysis that automates analytical model building.
- It is a branch of AI where systems learn from data, identify patterns, and make decisions with minimal human intervention.
- Examples: virtual assistants, commute predictions, video surveillance, social media services, spam/malware filtering, online customer support, and search result refining.
- **Exam Focus - Fill-in / MCQ**: Machine learning means machines learn from data patterns to make decisions automatically.
- **Practice Focus - Short Answer**: Explain Machine Learning.

## Neural Networks

- **Artificial Neural Network (人工神经网络)** is a computer program inspired by the organization of a real neural network, such as the human brain.
- It uses algorithms to recognize relationships in data through a process that mimics how the brain works.
- **Exam Focus - MCQ**: Neural networks are inspired by the **human brain (人脑)**.
- Examples include feedforward neural networks, recurrent neural networks, convolutional neural networks, and long/short-term memory networks.

## AI Frontiers

- **Robotics (机器人技术)**: building machines capable of physical tasks. Example: ElliQ can converse with patients, remind them to take medicine, and lead light activities.
- **Computer Vision (计算机视觉)**: equipping computers to interpret visual images. Examples: Amazon Go shelf cameras and Facebook DeepFace facial recognition.
- **Speech Recognition (语音识别)**: converting speech signals into digital data. Examples: Siri, Google Assistant, Galaxy assistant.
- **Natural Language Processing / NLP (自然语言处理)**: interaction between computers and humans using natural language. Examples: predictive text and Google RankBrain.
- **Exam Focus - MCQ**: Computer vision aims to equip computers to interpret visual images.
- **Exam Focus - MCQ**: Speech recognition converts speech into digital data.
- **Exam Focus - Fill-in**: RankBrain is linked to **Natural Language Processing (自然语言处理)**.
- **Practice Focus - Short Answer**: List AI research areas and development.

## Narrow AI

- **Narrow AI (狭义人工智能)** performs a specific task efficiently without general intelligence.
- Examples: movie recommendation systems, spam filters, virtual assistants, and search ranking.
- **Exam Focus - MCQ**: A streaming platform recommending movies based on viewing history is **Narrow AI**.

## Impacts of AI on Labor

- AI may automate repetitive tasks and some decision-making work.
- It raises questions about which jobs are most at risk, how blue-collar and white-collar work will change, and what new tasks will be created.
- **Exam Focus - Ethics Link**: AI replacing human workers and creating unemployment is an ethical concern.
