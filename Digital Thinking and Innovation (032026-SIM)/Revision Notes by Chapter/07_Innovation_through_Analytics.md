# 07 Innovation through Analytics

## Data vs Information

- **Data (数据)** is raw, unorganized facts that need to be processed.
- **Information (信息)** is data that has been processed, organized, structured, or presented in context to make it useful.
- **Exam Focus - Fill-in / Matching**: Raw, unorganized facts = **Data**.
- **Exam Focus - Fill-in / Matching**: Processed data used for decisions = **Information**.
- **Practice Focus - Short Answer**: Differentiate between Data and Information.

## Data Analytics

- **Data Analytics (数据分析)** is the science of analyzing raw data to make conclusions about information, enhance productivity, and support business gain.
- Extracted data is cleaned and categorized to analyze behavioral patterns.
- Data analytics techniques and tools vary depending on the organization or individual.
- Many processes are automated through mechanical processes and algorithms.

## Why Data Analytics Is Needed

- **Gather Hidden Insights (发现隐藏洞察)**: find useful patterns in data based on business needs.
- **Generate Reports (生成报告)**: pass useful information to teams and individuals for action.
- **Perform Market Analysis (进行市场分析)**: understand competitors' strengths and weaknesses.
- **Improve Business Requirements (改善业务需求)**: improve customer experience and business-customer requirements.
- **Exam Focus - MCQ**: Analytics helps businesses understand what happened, why it happened, and what may happen next.

## Examples of Data Analytics

- **Fraud Management Report (欺诈管理报告)**: used in banking to detect fraud transactions, hacking, and unauthorized account access.
- **Live Tracking Report (实时追踪报告)**: used by transport sectors such as Uber-like services to track vehicles, customer requests, payments, emergency alerts, and revenue.
- **Sales Report and Future Target Analysis (销售报告与未来目标分析)**: used to analyze sales, revenue, customer needs, and future targets.
- **Google Analytics Report (谷歌分析报告)**: tracks visit counts, user location, device type, and website access patterns.
- **Exam Focus - Matching**: Banking fraud detection = **Fraud Management Report**.

## Tools for Data Analytics

- **R programming (R 编程)**: statistics and data modeling.
- **Python (Python 编程)**: machine learning and visualization libraries such as scikit-learn, TensorFlow, Matplotlib, Pandas, and Keras.
- **Tableau Public (Tableau 可视化工具)**: visualizations, maps, dashboards, and real-time web updates.
- **QlikView (QlikView 分析工具)**: in-memory data processing, association, visualization, and compression.
- **SAS (SAS 分析环境)**: data manipulation and analytics from different sources.
- **OpenRefine / GoogleRefine (数据清洗工具)**: cleaning messy data, transforming data, and parsing website data.
- **Microsoft Excel (Excel 表格工具)**: pivot tables and internal data analysis.
- **RapidMiner (预测分析平台)**: data mining, text analytics, and machine learning.
- **KNIME (开源数据分析平台)**: modular data pipelines for analysis, modeling, reporting, and integration.
- **Apache Spark (大规模数据处理引擎)**: fast large-scale processing and machine learning pipelines.

## Data Analyst

- **Data Analyst (数据分析师)** is a professional who analyzes data using tools and techniques to gather insights.
- Data analysts translate numbers into plain English.
- Skills: statistics, data collection, data cleaning, data analysis, hidden insight discovery, data visualization, report generation, and machine learning.

## Big Data and Big Data Analytics

- **Big Data (大数据)** means large sets of machine-generated data, often unstructured and difficult to use with traditional RDBMS concepts.
- **Big Data Analytics (大数据分析)** is the complex process of examining large and varied data sets to uncover information that helps organizations make informed business decisions.
- **Exam Focus - Fill-in**: Large sets of machine-generated data = **Big Data**.
- **Exam Focus - Fill-in**: Collecting, analyzing, and interpreting large data volumes to extract meaningful insights = **Big Data Analytics**.

## Four Types of Big Data Analytics

- **Descriptive Analytics (描述性分析)**: learns from past behavior and answers "What has happened?"
- **Diagnostic Analytics (诊断性分析)**: root cause analysis; answers "Why did this happen?"
- **Predictive Analytics (预测性分析)**: uses historical data and customer insight to predict future events; answers "What will happen if...?"
- **Prescriptive Analytics (规范性分析 / 处方性分析)**: uses AI to understand the impact of future decisions and determine the best outcome; answers "What should we do?"
- **Exam Focus - Fill-in**: Prescriptive analytics uses AI to understand future decision impact and determine the best outcome.
- **Practice Focus - MCQ**: "How many cars did we sell last year?" = Descriptive.
- **Practice Focus - MCQ**: "Why did we only sell 1500 cars last year?" = Diagnostic.
- **Practice Focus - MCQ**: "If I run 30 advertising programs, how many cars can we sell?" = Predictive.
- **Practice Focus - MCQ**: "What do we do to sell 3000 cars?" = Prescriptive.

## Uses of Big Data Analytics

- **Customer acquisition and retention (客户获取与保留)**: Coca-Cola used a digital-led loyalty program and data strategy.
- **Marketing insights (营销洞察)**: Netflix recommends movies based on past search and watch data.
- **Risk management (风险管理)**: UOB used big data to reduce value-at-risk calculation time from many hours to minutes.
- **Innovation and product development (创新与产品开发)**: Amazon Fresh and Whole Foods use data-driven logistics and grocery purchasing insights.
- **Supply chain management (供应链管理)**: PepsiCo uses warehouse and POS inventory data to forecast production and shipment needs.
