# 08 Innovation through IoE

## Internet of Things

- **Internet of Things / IoT (物联网)** refers to real-world physical objects connected to the Internet and to other physical objects.
- IoT represents the dissolution of boundaries between the virtual and physical worlds.
- The primary purpose of IoT devices is not human communication, but sensing data, analyzing data, and autonomously controlling devices.
- **Online artifacts (联网物品)** include kitchen appliances, door locks, home alarms, weather sensors, automobiles, energy sensors, industrial control systems, smart watches, and smart home devices.
- **Exam Focus - Fill-in**: IoT devices connect and exchange data with other devices and systems over the **Internet (互联网)**.
- **Exam Focus - Fill-in / MCQ**: Main Internet users of IoT = **objects (物体)**.
- **Practice Focus - Short Answer**: Explain Internet of Things.

## Digitization of Everyday Objects

- Connected objects include fitness bracelets, glucose monitors, coffee makers, door locks, RFID retail items, vehicle sensors, manufacturing robots, and security systems.
- IoT improves daily activities by monitoring conditions, collecting data automatically, controlling devices remotely, and making services more convenient.
- **Practice Focus - Short Answer**: Explain how IoT improves daily activities.

## Cyber-Physical Systems and Autonomy

- **Cyber-Physical Systems (网络物理系统)** combine computation, networking, and physical processes.
- Examples include 3D printing, augmented reality, and advanced robotics.
- **Autonomy (自主性)** means systems can generate, exchange, and consume data with minimal human intervention.
- **Machine-to-Machine / M2M communication (机器对机器通信)** means devices communicate with each other without constant human control.
- **Exam Focus - Fill-in**: IoT autonomy refers to systems generating, exchanging, and consuming data with minimal **human (人类)** intervention.

## Benefits of IoT

- **Communication (通信)**: encourages device-to-device communication, also called M2M communication.
- **Automation and Control (自动化与控制)**: physical objects can be digitally and centrally controlled through wireless infrastructure.
- **Information (信息)**: more data supports better decisions.
- **Monitoring (监控)**: devices collect information that was previously difficult to collect.
- **Time (时间效率)**: machine-to-machine interaction improves efficiency and produces faster results.
- **Money (成本节省)**: better surveillance of devices helps manage energy, resources, bottlenecks, breakdowns, and damages.
- **Better Quality of Life (更好的生活质量)**: IoT increases comfort, convenience, and management.
- **Exam Focus - Matching**: Benefits of IoT include **communication, automation, and control**.
- **Practice Focus - Short Answer**: List the benefits of IoT.

## Issues Evolving IoT

- **Compatibility (兼容性)**: devices may not work together or require extra software.
- **Complexity (复杂性)**: deployment can be difficult because of prerequisites, requirements, and multiple systems.
- **Privacy (隐私)**: device data such as location, health, and behavior may expose personal life.
- **Security (安全)**: hacked IoT devices may be controlled by attackers or used in larger attacks.
- **Safety (安全性 / 人身安全)**: device failure, overheating, or wrong control can create physical risk.
- **Exam Focus - Matching**: Privacy is a main concern in IoE.
- **Practice Focus - MCQ**: Hacked IoT access control = Security issue; location tracker exposing location = Privacy issue; device overheating = Safety issue.

## Internet of Everything

- **Internet of Everything / IoE (万物互联)** refers to all the connections that can be made when people, objects, animals, systems, and data are connected.
- IoE depends on massive data from computerized everyday objects, and only AI systems may be able to manage the scale.
- IoE may create a fully electronic ecosystem where people, objects, animals, and even near space become smart and connected.
- **Exam Focus - Matching / Fill-in**: Technology allowing shoes, books, objects, and systems to connect = **Internet of Everything**.
- **Practice Focus - Short Answer**: Explain Internet of Everything.

## IoT vs IoE

- **IoT (物联网)** focuses mainly on connected physical objects and devices.
- **IoE (万物互联)** is broader: it includes people, objects, animals, systems, processes, and massive data connections.
- 中文记忆：IoT = things connected; IoE = everything connected.
