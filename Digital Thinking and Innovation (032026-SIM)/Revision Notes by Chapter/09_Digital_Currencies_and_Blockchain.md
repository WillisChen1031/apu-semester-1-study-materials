# 09 Digital Currencies and Blockchain

## Digital Currency

- **Digital Currency / E-money (数字货币 / 电子货币)** is money that exists only electronically or on the Internet.
- It fills the uses of traditional money, but issuance, transfer, and record-keeping are digital.
- Digital currency is intangible and does not require physical cash.
- **Exam Focus - MCQ**: A currency existing only in digital form = **Digital Currency**.
- **Practice Focus - MCQ**: Digital currency exists only electronically.

## Advantages of Digital Currency

- **Streamlined alternative (更简化的替代方式)**: easier and more convenient than traditional payment.
- **Peer-to-peer transactions (点对点交易)**: users can transfer value directly.
- **Instantaneous and low-cost payments (即时且低成本支付)**.
- **Better record-keeping and transparency (更好的记录保存与透明度)**.
- **Global payment support (全球支付支持)**: useful for online and cross-border transactions.
- **Exam Focus - Short Answer**: Explain three advantages of digital currency.
- **Exam Focus - MCQ**: An advantage of digital currency is **instant transactions (即时交易)**.
- **Practice Focus - MCQ**: Major benefit = faster and cheaper transactions.

## Cryptocurrency

- **Cryptocurrency (加密货币)** is a type of digital or virtual currency secured by **cryptography (密码学)**.
- Cryptocurrency makes counterfeiting and **double-spending (双重支付)** nearly impossible.
- Many cryptocurrencies are decentralized networks based on **Blockchain (区块链)** technology.
- Examples: Bitcoin, Litecoin, Ripple, Ethereum, Dogecoin.
- **Exam Focus - Fill-in**: Cryptocurrency uses **cryptography (密码学)** to secure transactions and control creation of new units.
- **Exam Focus - MCQ**: Cryptocurrency differs from traditional digital currency because it is **decentralized (去中心化)**.
- **Practice Focus - Short Answer**: Explain cryptocurrency.

## Double-Spending

- **Double-spending (双重支付)** is the problem of spending the same digital money twice.
- Bitcoin solved this issue by using timestamped cryptographic blocks and blockchain records.
- **Exam Focus - MCQ**: Spending the same money twice = **Double-spending**.

## Advantages of Cryptocurrency

- **Built-in scarcity (内置稀缺性)** may support value and offer inflation protection.
- **Loosening of government currency monopolies (削弱政府货币垄断)** provides a means of exchange outside direct central bank control.
- **Self-interested, self-policing communities (自利且自我监督的社区)**: miners are rewarded and therefore help maintain accurate records.
- **Robust privacy protections (强隐私保护)**: users may use pseudonyms instead of real identity information.
- **Harder for governments to exact financial retribution (政府较难冻结或没收)** compared with bank accounts.
- **Fewer barriers and lower costs for international transactions (国际交易障碍和成本更低)**.
- **Generally cheaper than traditional electronic transactions (通常比传统电子交易便宜)** because fewer third-party processors are needed.

## Cons of Cryptocurrency

- **Lack of regulation (监管不足)** can support black market activity.
- **Tax evasion risk (逃税风险)** because cryptocurrencies may exist outside direct national control.
- **Data loss risk (数据丢失风险)**: users may lose private keys or access.
- **High price volatility and manipulation (高价格波动和操纵风险)**.
- **Difficulty exchanging for fiat currency (难以兑换法币)** for less popular cryptocurrencies.
- **Limited chargebacks or refunds (退款和撤销机制有限)**.
- **Environmental impact of mining (挖矿环境影响)** due to high computing power and energy usage.
- **Practice Focus - MCQ**: Major cryptocurrency investment risks include volatility, hacking/theft, and lack of regulation.

## Digital Currency vs Cryptocurrency

- **Digital currency (数字货币)** is a broad category of electronic money. It may be centralized, such as bank e-money or a CBDC.
- **Cryptocurrency (加密货币)** is a type of digital currency secured by cryptography and usually decentralized through blockchain.
- **CBDC / Central Bank Digital Currency (中央银行数字货币)** is issued and controlled by a central bank, so it is more centralized than Bitcoin-like cryptocurrencies.
- **Exam Focus - MCQ**: Cryptocurrencies are decentralized, while central bank digital currencies are centralized.

## Blockchain

- **Blockchain (区块链)** is the master ledger that records and stores all prior transactions and activity, validating ownership of all units of currency at any given point in time.
- It records the entire transaction history to date.
- A blockchain contains a finite number of transactions that increases over time.
- A cryptocurrency transaction is not finalized until it is added to the blockchain, and after finalization it is usually irreversible.
- Blockchain is a peer-to-peer system with no central authority managing data flow.
- **Full nodes (完整节点)** are independent users in multiple locations that store and validate blockchain data.
- **Exam Focus - MCQ / Fill-in**: The master ledger that records all prior transactions and validates ownership = **Blockchain**.

## Bitcoin

- **Bitcoin (比特币)** was the first cryptocurrency and helped solve the double-spend problem.
- Bitcoin functions as a **medium of exchange (交换媒介)**, **unit of account (记账单位)**, and **store of value (价值储藏)**.
- Bitcoin is scarce, unduplicable, and portable.
- To alter Bitcoin records, an attacker would need to redo the entire blockchain.

## Miners

- **Miners (矿工)** are record-keepers for cryptocurrency communities and indirect arbiters of currency value.
- They use computing power to verify the completeness, accuracy, and security of blockchains.
- Mining can generate new cryptocurrency by solving complex mathematical problems.
- **Practice Focus - MCQ**: Generating new cryptocurrency by solving complex mathematical problems = **Mining (挖矿)**.

## Future of Digital Currency

- Digital currencies are supported by digital infrastructure and wider high-speed Internet access.
- Blockchain advantages have attracted central banks to CBDC projects.
- CBDCs use centralized distributed ledger technology, allowing central banks to issue and control money similarly to fiat currency systems.
