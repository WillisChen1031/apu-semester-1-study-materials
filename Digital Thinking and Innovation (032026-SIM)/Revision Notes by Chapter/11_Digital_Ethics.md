# 11 Digital Ethics

## Ethics, Morality, Law, and Computer Ethics

- **Ethics (伦理 / 道德规范)** is a set of beliefs about right and wrong behavior within a society.
  中文：伦理是社会中关于行为对错的一套信念。
- **Morality (道德观)** refers to shared social conventions about right and wrong that form an established consensus.
  中文：道德是社会共同认可的对错标准和共识。
- **Law (法律)** is a system of rules enforced by countries and institutions telling people what is acceptable and unacceptable.
  中文：法律是国家或机构强制执行的规则，规定什么可接受、什么不可接受。
- **Computer Ethics (计算机伦理)** is the moral guidelines that govern the use of computers and information systems.
  中文：计算机伦理是使用电脑和信息系统时应遵守的道德准则。
- **Digital Ethics (数字伦理)** concerns rules and moral guidelines governing interpersonal behavior mediated by computer technology.
  中文：数字伦理关注由计算机技术媒介产生的人际行为规则和道德准则。
- **Exam Focus - Short Answer**: Describe ethics, morality, and computer ethics.
  中文：简答题分别写：ethics = beliefs；morality = shared conventions；computer ethics = guidelines for computer/IS use。
- **Exam Focus - MCQ**: Moral guidelines governing use of computers and information systems = **Computer Ethics** or **Digital Ethics**, depending on wording.
  中文：如果题目说 computers and information systems，常选 Computer Ethics；说 mediated by computer technology，常选 Digital Ethics。
- **Exam Focus - MCQ**: A system of rules enforced by countries and institutions = **Law**.
  中文：enforced by countries/institutions，就是 Law。

## Virtue and Vice

- **Virtue (美德)** is a habit that inclines people to do what is acceptable.
  中文：美德让人倾向做被接受、正确的事。
- **Vice (恶习)** is a habit of unacceptable behavior.
  中文：恶习让人倾向做不被接受的事。
- Virtues and vices define an individual's moral values.
  中文：美德和恶习共同反映一个人的道德价值。
- **Exam Focus - Fill-in**: Habit that inclines people to do what is not acceptable = **Vice**.
  中文：habit + not acceptable，答案 Vice。

## Concerns About Ethical Use of IT

- Workplace email and Internet monitoring.
  中文：工作场所监控邮件和网络使用。
- Software piracy and copyright violations.
  中文：软件盗版和版权违法。
- Unsolicited email, spoofing, spamming, and phishing.
  中文：垃圾邮件、伪造身份、群发垃圾信息、钓鱼攻击。
- Hacker and cracker theft.
  中文：黑客/破解者盗窃。
- Student plagiarism.
  中文：学生抄袭。
- Malware, cookies, and spyware used to illegally track users.
  中文：恶意软件、cookies、间谍软件非法追踪用户。
- Unauthorized access and digital privacy violations.
  中文：未授权访问和数字隐私侵犯。

## Code of Ethics

- **Code of Ethics (道德规范 / 伦理守则)** is a document that states an organization's mission, values, employee conduct rules, and ethical principles.
  中文：道德规范文件写明组织使命、价值观、员工行为规则和伦理原则。
- Breaking the code can result in termination.
  中文：违反道德规范可能导致解雇。
- It provides clear behavioral rules and a warning before problems occur.
  中文：它提前告诉员工哪些行为可以、哪些不可以。
- ACM examples: avoid harm, be fair, do not discriminate, honor property rights, credit intellectual property, respect privacy, and honor confidentiality.
  中文：ACM 例子：避免伤害、公平、不歧视、尊重产权、注明知识产权、尊重隐私、保密。
- **Exam Focus - Fill-in**: Code of Ethics documents mission, values, and employee conduct rules.
  中文：mission + values + employee conduct rules，答案 Code of Ethics。
- **Exam Focus - Short Answer**: Explain why it is important to act according to a code of ethics.
  中文：简答题写：指导行为、减少不道德行为、保护组织和客户、提高专业性。

## Profession, Professionals, and Professionalism

- **Profession (专业)** requires specialized knowledge and intensive academic preparation.
  中文：专业需要专门知识和系统学术训练。
- **Professionals (专业人士)** possess advanced training and experience, exercise discretion and judgment, and keep up with developments in their field.
  中文：专业人士有高级训练和经验，能做判断，并持续跟进行业发展。
- **Professionalism (专业精神)** means acting responsibly according to professional standards.
  中文：专业精神就是按专业标准负责任地行动。
- **Integrity (诚信)** means acting according to personal principles and extending respect, consideration, and moral values to others.
  中文：诚信是按照原则行事，并尊重、体谅他人。
- **Exam Focus - Fill-in**: People with advanced training and specialized knowledge = **Professionals**.
  中文：advanced training + specialized knowledge，答案 Professionals。

## Business Ethics and CSR

- **Business Ethics (商业伦理)** is increasingly important because complex work environments make ethical principles difficult to apply consistently.
  中文：商业伦理重要，因为复杂工作环境中很难始终一致地应用伦理原则。
- Pressure to maintain revenue and profits can increase unethical behavior.
  中文：保持收入和利润的压力可能导致不道德行为。
- Unethical behavior can cause financial loss, criminal lawsuits, civil lawsuits, and reputation damage.
  中文：不道德行为会带来财务损失、刑事/民事诉讼、声誉损害。
- **Corporate Social Responsibility / CSR (企业社会责任)** means an organization takes responsibility for the impact of its actions on the environment, community, and employee welfare.
  中文：CSR 指组织对环境、社区和员工福利承担责任。

## Ethics Training

- **Ethics Training (伦理培训)** educates employees on how to apply the code of ethics in real life.
  中文：伦理培训教员工如何在真实工作场景应用道德规范。
- **Exam Focus - Short Answer**: Three goals of ethics training:
  中文：简答题常考 ethics training goals：
  1. Help employees understand and apply the Code of Ethics in real workplace situations.
     中文：帮助员工理解并应用道德规范。
  2. Encourage ethical decision-making and responsible behavior.
     中文：鼓励做道德决策和负责任行为。
  3. Encourage reporting of misconduct, show effective reporting methods, and reassure employees that reports will be acted on without retaliation.
     中文：鼓励举报不当行为，教举报方式，并保证不会被报复。
- You can also mention preventing unethical actions such as fraud, bribery, conflict of interest, and misuse of information.
  中文：也可补充：防止欺诈、贿赂、利益冲突、滥用信息。

## Ethical Decision-Making

- Ethical decisions in computer crime may include whether to prosecute criminals, keep a low profile to avoid bad publicity, or inform affected customers.
  中文：面对计算机犯罪，公司要决定是否起诉、是否低调处理、是否通知受影响客户。
- Firms must decide how many resources to spend on safeguards and what actions to take when software is vulnerable to hacking.
  中文：公司还要决定投入多少资源做安全防护，以及发现漏洞时如何处理。
- Security safeguards may increase operating costs, but weak safeguards increase ethical and legal risk.
  中文：安全措施会增加成本，但防护不足会增加伦理和法律风险。

## Ethical Issues

- **Ethical Issues (伦理问题)** are situations where moral principles conflict and careful decision-making is required.
  中文：伦理问题是道德原则发生冲突，需要谨慎决策的情况。
- **Software Piracy (软件盗版)**: unauthorized copying or use of software.
  中文：未经授权复制或使用软件。
- **Business Software Alliance / BSA (商业软件联盟)**: trade group aiming to stop unauthorized copying of software.
  中文：BSA 是阻止软件非法复制的行业组织。
- **Trade Secret (商业秘密)**: information with economic value, development effort/cost, and uniqueness or novelty.
  中文：商业秘密是有经济价值、投入成本开发、并具有一定独特性的信息。
- **Whistle-blowing (吹哨)**: an employee's effort to expose negligent, illegal, unethical, or abusive acts that threaten public interest.
  中文：吹哨是员工揭露威胁公共利益的疏忽、违法、不道德或滥用行为。
- **Conflict of Interest (利益冲突)**: conflict between an employee's self-interest and the client's or organization's interest.
  中文：个人利益与客户/组织利益冲突。
- **Fraud (欺诈)**: obtaining goods, services, or property through deception or trickery.
  中文：通过欺骗获得商品、服务或财产。
- **Misrepresentation (虚假陈述)**: misstatement or incomplete statement of a material fact.
  中文：对重要事实作错误或不完整陈述。
- **Breach of Contract (违约)**: one party fails to meet contract terms.
  中文：一方没有履行合同条款。
- **Exam Focus - Fill-in**: BSA aims to stop unauthorized **pirating/copying (盗版/复制)** of software.
  中文：BSA 的目标是阻止 unauthorized copying/pirating。
- **Exam Focus - Fill-in**: Information with economic value and uniqueness = **Trade Secret**.
  中文：economic value + uniqueness，对应 Trade Secret。
- **Exam Focus - Fill-in / MCQ**: Employee exposing illegal or unethical acts = **Whistle-blower / Whistle-blowing**.
  中文：exposes illegal/unethical acts，对应 Whistle-blower。
- **Exam Focus - Fill-in**: Obtaining goods or services through deception = **Fraud**.
  中文：deception/trickery 获得东西，答案 Fraud。
- **Exam Focus - MCQ**: Sharing confidential customer information without permission = **Unethical**.
  中文：未经允许分享客户机密信息，是不道德行为。

## Examples of Bad Ethics

- **Conflict of interest (利益冲突)**: an employee runs a side business competing with their employer.
  中文：员工开副业和雇主竞争。
- **Insider trading (内幕交易)**: a CEO sells shares after learning about financial loss before the information becomes public.
  中文：内部人员利用未公开信息交易股票。
- **Deceptive advertising (欺骗性广告)**: misleading customers, such as false environmental claims.
  中文：广告误导消费者，例如虚假环保宣传。
- **Software piracy (软件盗版)**: unauthorized copying or use of software.
  中文：未经授权复制或使用软件。
- **Plagiarism (剽窃)**: presenting someone else's work as your own without credit.
  中文：把别人的作品当成自己的，不注明来源。
- **Confidentiality breach (泄露机密)**: disclosing private or sensitive information without permission.
  中文：未经允许泄露私人或敏感信息。
- **Exam Focus - Short Answer**: Provide three examples of bad ethics.
  中文：简答题任写三个例子并简单解释即可。

## Bribes and Gifts

- **Bribe (贿赂)** is made in secret, offers something valuable to gain undue influence or unfair advantage, is unethical and often illegal, and normally involves a third party.
  中文：贿赂通常秘密进行，目的是用有价值的东西换取不正当影响或优势，通常违法/不道德。
- **Gift (礼物)** is made openly, given directly from giver to receiver, and has no expectation of future favor.
  中文：礼物公开给予，直接从赠送者到接收者，不期待未来回报。
- **Exam Focus - Short Answer**: Contrast bribes and gifts.
  中文：比较题从 secret/open、expect favor/no expectation、illegal/legal、third party/direct giver-receiver 写。
- **Exam Focus - MCQ**: Paying money to a superior for promotion = **Bribery**.
  中文：给上司钱换升职，就是 Bribery。
- **Exam Focus - MCQ**: Accepting gifts from a supplier in exchange for favorable decisions creates **Conflict of Interest**.
  中文：收供应商礼物换偏袒决策，会造成利益冲突。

## Digital Privacy, AI Ethics, and Programming Ethics

- **Lack of Data Privacy (缺乏数据隐私)** occurs when a company collects user data without clearly disclosing how it will be used or stored.
  中文：公司收集用户数据却不说明如何使用/储存，就是缺乏数据隐私。
- **Algorithmic Bias (算法偏见)** occurs when an AI or ML system produces unfair results because of biased training data.
  中文：训练数据有偏，会导致算法对某些群体不公平。
- **Algorithmic Transparency (算法透明度)** means users and stakeholders can understand how automated decisions are made.
  中文：算法透明指用户和相关方能理解自动化决策如何产生。
- **Insider Threat (内部威胁)** occurs when someone inside an organization abuses access for personal gain.
  中文：内部人员滥用权限谋取私利，就是内部威胁。
- **Unethical Programming / Malware Development (不道德编程 / 恶意软件开发)** includes intentionally adding backdoors or code to access user data without consent.
  中文：故意加后门或代码偷看用户数据，属于不道德编程/恶意软件开发。
- **Exam Focus - MCQ**: Biased training data rejecting a demographic group = **Algorithmic Bias**.
  中文：biased training data 导致不公平结果，答案 Algorithmic Bias。
- **Exam Focus - MCQ**: Company collecting app data without disclosure = **Lack of Data Privacy**.
  中文：收集数据但不披露用途/储存方式，答案 Lack of Data Privacy。
- **Exam Focus - MCQ**: IT administrator abusing company access for personal gain = **Insider Threat**.
  中文：内部 IT 人员滥用权限，答案 Insider Threat。
- **Exam Focus - MCQ**: Adding a backdoor to access user data = **Malware Development**.
  中文：故意加后门偷数据，答案 Malware Development。

## Regulations and Legal Notes

- **GDPR / General Data Protection Regulation (通用数据保护条例)** protects the digital privacy of European citizens.
  中文：GDPR 保护欧洲公民数字隐私。
- **PDPA 2010 (个人数据保护法 2010)** is a Malaysian personal data protection law.
  中文：PDPA 2010 是马来西亚个人数据保护法。
- **DMCA (数字千年版权法)** relates to copyright protection.
  中文：DMCA 与版权保护有关。
- **CCPA (加州消费者隐私法案)** relates to California consumer privacy.
  中文：CCPA 与加州消费者隐私有关。
- **Exam Focus - MCQ**: Regulation specifically designed to protect digital privacy of European citizens = **GDPR**.
  中文：European citizens + digital privacy，答案 GDPR。
