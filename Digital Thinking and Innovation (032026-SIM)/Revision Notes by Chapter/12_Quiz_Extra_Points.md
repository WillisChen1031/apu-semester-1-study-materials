## Estonia Digital Government

- **X-Road (X-Road 数据交换层)** is the core decentralized data exchange layer that enables secure data sharing in Estonia.  
  中文：X-Road 是爱沙尼亚电子政府的核心去中心化数据交换层，用来让政府、机构和系统之间安全共享数据。
- **Exam Focus - MCQ**: Core decentralized data exchange layer enabling secure data sharing in Estonia = **X-Road**.  
  中文：题目出现 Estonia + decentralized data exchange + secure data sharing，答案选 X-Road。
- **e-ID system (电子身份系统)** is used for digital identity, but it is not the data exchange layer.  
  中文：e-ID 是电子身份，不是数据交换层。
- **i-Voting platform (网上投票平台)** is for online voting, not general data exchange.  
  中文：i-Voting 是网上投票平台，不是通用数据交换层。
- **Digital Signature Centre (数字签名中心)** relates to digital signatures, not the core decentralized data exchange layer.  
  中文：Digital Signature Centre 和数字签名有关，不是 X-Road 那种数据交换层。

## Multitasking Extra Points

- **Multitasking on complex tasks (复杂任务多任务处理)** often increases the total time needed to complete tasks.  
  中文：复杂任务中频繁切换注意力会拖慢速度，所以总完成时间增加。
- **Exam Focus - MCQ**: Common consequence of multitasking on complex tasks = **increased total time to complete tasks**.  
  中文：复杂任务 + multitasking 的后果，选完成时间增加。
- Frequent multitasking commonly impairs **short-term memory / working memory (短期记忆 / 工作记忆)**.  
  中文：频繁多任务会影响短期记忆，因为大脑要不断切换和暂存信息。
- Multitasking can be effective when tasks are **simple, repetitive, and do not require intense focus (简单、重复、不需要高度专注)**.  
  中文：只有简单重复任务比较适合多任务；高专注任务不适合。

## Whole Brain Model

- **Whole Brain Model (全脑模型)** divides thinking styles into **four (四个)** parts.  
  中文：Whole Brain Model 把思维风格分成四个部分。
- **Exam Focus - MCQ**: Thinking styles in the Whole Brain Model are divided into **four** parts.  
  中文：问 Whole Brain Model 分几部分，答案 Four。

## Quiz Conflict Notes

- Some online quiz answers may conflict with general reasoning or earlier tutorial wording. Use the quiz feedback as the standard for that quiz attempt.  
  中文：如果线上 quiz 判分和一般理解不一致，当前 quiz 复习时以系统反馈为准。
- Example: For the delivery-speed question, the system marked **Delay deliveries until all orders are grouped** as correct, even though **real-time logistics tracking** also sounds reasonable from a general digital-thinking perspective.  
  中文：例如配送速度那题，系统标准答案是“先把订单分组再配送”，虽然一般数字思维角度“实时物流追踪”也合理。
- Example: For **Willingness to fail**, the system marked **To avoid failure is to avoid success** as correct.  
  中文：愿意失败那题，系统答案是“避免失败就是避免成功”，意思是不能因为怕失败而不尝试。
