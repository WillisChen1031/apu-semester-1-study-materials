## Question 1

**Question:**  
Ethics training is important in organizations to educate employees how to apply the Code of Ethics in real life to encourage ethical behavior. Explain clearly THREE (3) goals of ethics training.

**Answer:**

1. To help employees understand and apply the Code of Ethics in real workplace situations.  
2. To encourage ethical decision-making and responsible behaviour at work.  
3. To prevent unethical actions such as fraud, bribery, conflict of interest, or misuse of information.

---

## Question 2

**Question:**  
Discuss any FOUR (4) methods to secure your computer or electronic devices from any form of unauthorized access.

**Answer:**

1. Enable two-factor authentication (2FA).  
2. Use strong and unique passwords.  
3. Use VPN when accessing sensitive systems or public networks.  
4. Install and use antivirus software.
