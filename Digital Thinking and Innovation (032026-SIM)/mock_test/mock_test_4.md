## Question 41

**Question:**  
Explain TWO (2) benefits of digital culture on modern healthcare services and explain ONE (1) example of the benefits.  
(6 marks)

**Answer:**

1. **Improved access to healthcare**  
Digital culture allows patients to use online consultation, health apps, and telemedicine. This helps patients get medical advice without always visiting a hospital.

2. **Faster communication and information sharing**  
Doctors and hospital staff can share patient information, test results, and treatment updates more quickly through digital systems. This improves the speed and quality of healthcare services.

**Example:**  
A patient in a rural area can use a telemedicine app or video call to consult a hospital doctor, receive advice, and follow up on treatment without travelling to the hospital.

## Question 42

**Question:**  
Explain digital collaboration and describe any FOUR (4) digital collaboration tools for hospital doctors to provide consultancy and advisory to their patients.  
(8 marks)

**Answer:**

**Digital collaboration** means using digital technologies to communicate, share information, and work together even when people are in different locations.

1. **Zoom**  
Doctors can use Zoom for video consultations with patients.

2. **Microsoft Teams**  
Doctors and hospital staff can discuss patient cases, share files, and hold online meetings.

3. **Google Meet**  
Doctors can provide remote medical advice through video calls.

4. **WhatsApp**  
Doctors or hospital staff can send appointment reminders, basic advice, and follow-up messages to patients.

## Question 43

**Question:**  
Explain THREE (3) advantages of digital currency.  
(6 marks)

**Answer:**

1. **Fast transactions**  
Digital currency allows users to send and receive money quickly, often instantly.

2. **Convenience**  
Users can make payments using mobile phones or online wallets without carrying physical cash.

3. **Global payment support**  
Digital currency can support online and cross-border transactions, making payment easier for international users.
